About me

My name is Amit Pollak.
I began my art journey 6 years ago.
All my life, I’ve been interested in drawing, but for some reason,I was afraid to try it out.

One day, suddenly it happened, I don't know how, I just took a pencil and drew a figure. This figure has changed my life.

At a certain point in my life, I started to look closely at every experience that occurred at my door.
To examine with passion and ask questions about things that are obvious and question them.

That is a big reason why I love art.

I find that art is a way to express yourself and to show your feelings and thoughts in a creative way.
Making an art piece usually puts you in an observer's perspective.

You need to look at how the thing that you are now making is shaped and what emotion or reason is behind it.
Understanding the line’s structure, shade, light, different body movements, and more.
Art gives a wider perspective and helps you to see things in a new way.
